How to Manage Your Blog
🚀 Adding a New Blog Post (Full Workflow)
1
Upload your thumbnail image to public_html/blog-images/ on GoDaddy (e.g. my-post.jpg)
2
Come to this admin panel → New Post tab → fill all the fields
3
Click Generate JSON Entry → copy the output
4
Download blogs.json from your GoDaddy server
5
Open it in Notepad → find the last {"{"} closing brace → add a comma after it → paste your new entry
6
Save and re-upload blogs.json to GoDaddy → your post is live instantly! ✅
🖼️ Image Guidelines for Best Results
1
Recommended thumbnail size: 1200 × 630px (same as Facebook/WhatsApp share size)
2
Format: .jpg or .webp for smallest file size. Keep under 200KB.
3
Name your files clearly: python-basics-durgapur.jpg (no spaces, lowercase)
4
Upload to public_html/blog-images/ folder. If that folder doesn't exist, create it.
5
In the form, type: blog-images/your-image.jpg
