No. Level 1 starts from absolute basics. We have trained school students, homemakers and professionals with zero prior coding. All you need is a laptop and curiosity.

Physical classes in Asansol and Durgapur at 3/14 Central Park, City Centre. Online sessions via Google Meet or Zoom are also available.

The complete 7-level program takes 5 to 6 months. Each level takes 3 to 6 weeks with flexible evening and weekend batches.
